Researchers Warn AI Is Crowding Hedge-Fund Trades and Shortening Signal Lifespans

Wall Street researchers cautioned on July 1 that widespread AI adoption is making trades more crowded and risk harder to control. NYU researchers analyzing nearly one million institutional holdings found portfolio similarity rising fastest among heavy AI users, while separate work suggests a profitable signal that once lasted five to seven years now sees its excess return halve in about 18 months. Why it matters: If AI compresses the half-life of alpha and pushes managers into the same positions, diversification across "uncorrelated" quant funds may be more illusory than allocators assume.
