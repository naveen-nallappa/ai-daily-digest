Gemini 3.5 Pro Slips to July as Alphabet's AI Setbacks Erase Roughly $270 Billion

Google has pushed Gemini 3.5 Pro's general-availability launch from June into July, leaving its next flagship in limited Vertex AI enterprise preview while it tunes coding and agent performance. The delay landed the same week several senior Gemini researchers left for rivals, including DeepMind vice president and Nobel laureate John Jumper, who is joining Anthropic. Amazon Weighs Selling Trainium AI Chips Directly to Outside Data Centers

Amazon is in talks to sell its custom Trainium AI accelerators for use in other companies' data centers, Bloomberg reported June 18 — a break from a decade of AWS-only distribution and a direct challenge to Nvidia. CEO Andy Jassy flagged the possibility in his April shareholder letter, estimating Amazon's chip unit would run at roughly $50 billion annually as a standalone business. The company also unveiled AWS Factories, putting Trainium and Nvidia GPUs on-premise inside customer facilities.

Why it matters: A credible third merchant-silicon supplier alongside Nvidia and AMD would widen buyers' options and pressure accelerator pricing across the buildout. PMs in semis should watch whether Trainium escapes AWS's walled garden, and allocators modeling the AI capex cycle should factor cheaper inference hardware into margin assumptions for both chipmakers and the hyperscalers deploying it.
European Commission

EU AI Act Transparency Rules Take Effect August 2 for AI Interactions

From August 2, 2026, the EU AI Act requires firms to tell people when they are interacting with an AI system and to machine-label AI-generated or manipulated content, including deepfakes. The Commission's Digital Omnibus simplification package, which reached political agreement on May 7, eases documentation for smaller firms but shortens the grace period for content-transparency tooling to three months, setting a December 2, 2026 deadline. Draft guidance on the transparency obligations remains in public consultation.

Why it matters: Advisor chatbots, AI meeting notes, and marketing content touching EU clients now need disclosure and labeling controls within weeks. Compliance and vendor-selection teams should confirm that any client-facing AI flags itself and watermarks generated output, and press wealthtech providers for documented August-2 readiness rather than roadmap promises before deploying agents into regulated workflows.

Advisor CRM Launches Ember, an AI Client-Engagement Layer With Advisor Review

Advisor CRM on June 23 launched Ember, an AI-powered client-experience tool that replaces scattered intake forms and manual data entry with a single branded link clients use on any device. BMO and REX List 3x Long and Short AI ETNs Aimed at Daily Traders

BMO and REX Shares began trading MicroSectors 3x Long (AIQU) and -3x Short (AIQD) Artificial Intelligence ETNs on NYSE Arca on June 2. Each note offers three-times daily leveraged or inverse exposure to the BITA AI Leaders Select index of 25 US-listed AI companies. As exchange-traded notes, they are unsecured debt obligations of BMO maturing in 2046, carrying issuer credit risk alongside the embedded leverage and daily reset mechanics.

Why it matters: Triple-leveraged single-theme ETNs are built for day-trading, not allocation — daily resets cause decay that erodes returns over any multi-day hold, especially in volatile AI names. Advisors fielding client demand for these tickers should flag both the compounding decay and BMO counterparty exposure, and steer longer-horizon AI exposure toward unleveraged funds rather than structured notes.
