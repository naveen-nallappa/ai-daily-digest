Bloomberg

Wall Street Researchers Warn AI Is Crowding Trades and Masking Risk

A July 1 Bloomberg review of recent studies found AI adoption could make hedge fund trading riskier: models push investors into the same crowded positions, shorten the lifespan of profitable signals, take more risk than intended, and can be manipulated through the information they ingest. The warnings land as banks roll out AI assistants and funds weave large language models into research and execution across the industry.

Why it matters: "AI-driven" is now a risk factor, not just a selling point. Allocators should ask quant and multi-strategy managers how AI signals are governed, how correlated their models are with peers using similar data, and how positions behave when many funds react to the same headline at once — crowding and manipulation risks that standard track records will not reveal.
