Mercer Survey Finds 55% of Asset Managers Use AI, Only 5% Autonomously

Mercer's 2026 AI in Asset Management survey found 55% of managers have integrated AI into at least one investment process, 27% are at pilot or proof-of-concept, and 18% report no integration yet.
