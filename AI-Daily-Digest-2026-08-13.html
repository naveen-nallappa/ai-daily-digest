Anthropic courts investors ahead of a possible public listing this fall

Anthropic is meeting prospective investors to shore up confidence ahead of a possible public-market debut this fall, the Wall Street Journal reported August 12. A listing would be the first by a frontier AI lab and would follow a rapid buildout of Anthropic's commercial footprint, including the Theseus data-center venture with Macquarie and GIC announced August 11.

Foxconn's AI server business tops half of revenue for the first time

Foxconn reported second-quarter net profit of NT$59.97 billion (about $1.86 billion) on August 12, up 35% year over year and ahead of the NT$58.8 billion LSEG consensus. Cloud and networking products, including AI servers, reached 51% of revenue, crossing half for the first time; the iPhone-centered consumer segment fell to 29%. Capital spending is set to rise 30% in 2026, with Nvidia Vera Rubin production preparation in the third quarter and shipments targeted for the fourth.

Why it matters: The world's largest contract manufacturer now earns more from AI servers than from consumer electronics, hard evidence that hyperscaler capex is converting into supply-chain revenue. PMs holding hardware names should note management's warning that 2027 volumes hinge on TSMC CoWoS packaging capacity — the next binding constraint to underwrite.
Ars Technica

Google's Gemini app passes 1 billion monthly users, its fastest ramp ever

Google said August 12 that the Gemini app surpassed 1 billion monthly active users, its 14th product to reach the milestone and the fastest to get there. The figure covers the standalone app and web interface, with more than 100 million active users on iOS. Google says 63% of users interact by voice and the system generates over 150 million images daily. The mark comes weeks after OpenAI reported ChatGPT crossing the same threshold.

Why it matters: Consumer AI distribution is consolidating into a two-horse race, and both horses just hit a billion users. For equity PMs the question shifts from adoption to monetization per user — watch whether Alphabet discloses Gemini-attributable revenue in coming quarters, since that line will drive the next leg of the multiple debate.
