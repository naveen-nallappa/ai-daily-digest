China's AI Quant Funds Draw Record Inflows as They Beat Human Traders

China's quantitative hedge funds are pulling in record money as AI-driven strategies outrun discretionary managers, Bloomberg reported July 2. Why it matters: A rapid, performance-driven shift toward AI quant strategies concentrates flows and can crowd the same trades, a dynamic that has amplified drawdowns when signals unwind. Investors with China or global quant exposure should probe how differentiated each manager's data and models really are, and stress-test for correlated losses if crowded AI-driven positioning suddenly reverses.
