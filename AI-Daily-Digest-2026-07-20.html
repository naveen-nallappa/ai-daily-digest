Survey finds 77% of large asset managers now run firm-wide generative AI platforms

Substantive Research and Aiera surveyed 35 of the largest global asset managers and found 77% have organisation-wide generative AI deployments in place. The same share named broker research the most valuable input to pipe into internal AI systems as machine-readable feeds, ahead of earnings transcripts at 57% and market data at 42%. Broker and data licensing restrictions were the top barrier at 69%, followed by compliance and entitlements at 54%. Onboarding a model took four to six months for 37% of respondents.

Why it matters: The constraint on buy-side AI is contractual, not technical, and research budgets are where it bites. Firms should audit whether existing broker agreements permit machine ingestion before scoping any research-copilot project, and raise machine-readability rights explicitly at the next commission negotiation rather than assuming current terms cover it.
