A Barclays survey of 410 institutional investors, reported June 4, found nearly three-quarters of hedge fund respondents use AI daily, well ahead of long-only managers and asset owners. Why it matters: Hard adoption numbers give allocators a benchmark for what "AI-enabled" should mean in diligence.
