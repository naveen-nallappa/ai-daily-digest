Morgan Stanley Opens Its Workplace Wealth Platform to External AI Agents

Morgan Stanley said June 3 it will let clients' autonomous AI agents pull data directly from its ShareWorks and Equity Edge stock-administration platforms, bypassing interfaces built for humans. The connection runs over Model Context Protocol, the open standard for linking AI models to data sources. A handful of clients have early access; the bank plans to extend it to all 3,400 administration clients next year. The workplace channel gathered $1.2 trillion in assets as of April.

Why it matters: This is among the first cases of a major bank exposing client-facing data to third-party agents rather than just deploying AI internally, and MCP is fast becoming the integration layer firms standardize on. Platform and security teams evaluating advisor tech should treat MCP support as a near-term diligence criterion, and model how agent-to-platform access changes authentication and audit requirements.
