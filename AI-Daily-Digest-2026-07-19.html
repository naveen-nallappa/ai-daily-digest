China launches Shanghai-based World AI Cooperation Organization with 29 founding nations

Twenty-nine countries signed the founding agreement for the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization in Shanghai on July 16, and Xi Jinping formally unveiled the body at the World AI Conference on July 17. WAICO will be headquartered in Shanghai and is the first intergovernmental organization dedicated solely to AI governance. Founding members include Russia, Pakistan and Kazakhstan; the United States and major European economies did not sign. Xi framed the group around open-source access and capacity-building for developing economies.

Why it matters: A second governance bloc raises the odds that AI rules fragment along geopolitical lines rather than converge on a single standard. Firms deploying models across jurisdictions should expect divergent disclosure, data-residency and audit requirements by 2027, and technology teams should press vendors on which regimes their models are certified under before signing multi-year enterprise contracts.

Wells Fargo ships AI Teammate to advisors overseeing $2.4 trillion in client assets

Wells Fargo launched AI Teammate on July 15, a chat-based assistant embedded in its Advisor Gateway platform that lets advisors, client associates and support teams query products, workflows and processes in plain language and take action from the results. The tool is live across all Wealth and Investment Management advisor channels, including the independent FiNet network, covering roughly $2.4 trillion in client assets. Advisors at competing firms should benchmark their own retrieval tooling against plain-language platform search, and enterprise buyers should note Wells Fargo built into the gateway instead of adding another point solution.
