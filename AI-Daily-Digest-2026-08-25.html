OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 Sol developer pricing by more than 20% through November

OpenAI reduced prices on its frontier GPT-5.6 Sol model effective August 21, taking input to $4 per million tokens from $5 and output to $20 from $30. The cut runs three months, through November, and extends to Codex credits and eligible ChatGPT Work plans, while Pro, Plus and Business consumer subscriptions are unchanged. It is the second reduction in under a month across the GPT-5.6 family, and follows competitive pressure from Anthropic and lower-cost Chinese models.

Why it matters: Inference is deflating while the hardware underneath it inflates, so per-seat AI budgets set six months ago are conservative on software and light on infrastructure. Firms negotiating enterprise AI contracts should push for pricing that tracks published API rates rather than locking multi-year fixed fees, and revisit workflows shelved as too expensive to run at scale.

The SEC has issued subpoenas to banks that financed and cleared trades for Situational Awareness, the AI-focused hedge fund run by former OpenAI researcher Leopold Aschenbrenner, according to a New York Times report on August 24.
