Visa (BusinessWire)

Visa agrees to buy AI fraud-detection firm BioCatch for $2.4 billion

Visa said August 3 it will acquire BioCatch, an Israeli behavioral-biometrics firm, for $2.4 billion in cash from funds advised by Permira and other shareholders. BioCatch flags fraud by reading how users type, swipe, and hold their devices, and serves more than 350 banks across 21 countries, with coverage extending to 1.8 billion devices. Visa, which has directed over $13 billion toward fraud technology in five years, expects the deal to close in its fiscal second quarter of 2027.

Why it matters: Account takeovers and scams now cost the global economy over $1 trillion a year, and generative AI is industrializing them. A payments network buying behavioral biometrics signals where fraud defense is heading; wealth and asset platforms should press custodians and vendors on device-level authentication and upstream detection rather than leaning on static passwords and transaction rules.
