White House nears a voluntary frontier-model deal with a 30-day security review

The White House is finalizing a voluntary framework with OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google that would give federal agencies up to 30 days to review a new frontier model's national-security implications before public release. Built on a June 2 executive order, it directs the NSA to classify "covered frontier models" through a secret benchmarking process and establishes a government-industry cybersecurity clearinghouse. An announcement is expected before August 1; Meta is not part of the deal.

Why it matters: A pre-release review window could add weeks to frontier-model launch timelines, affecting when new capabilities reach the enterprise tools advisors and PMs rely on.
