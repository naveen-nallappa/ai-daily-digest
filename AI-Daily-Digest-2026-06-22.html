Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro Faces a Self-Imposed End-of-June Launch Deadline

At Google I/O on May 19, Sundar Pichai previewed Gemini 3.5 Pro and told developers "give us until next month," but the model remained in limited Vertex AI preview as of June 21, leaving roughly a week to hit its end-of-June target. Pro promises a two-million-token context window, a "Deep Think" extended-reasoning mode, and frontier multimodal capability. Deep Think is gated to Google's $250-a-month Ultra tier. Why it matters: Another frontier model reaching general availability widens the multi-model rosters that advisory and asset-management technology teams now maintain. Zocks Launches 'Client Queries' to Mine Advisor Books for Growth Gaps

Zocks unveiled Client Queries on June 16, an agentic feature that lets advisors and firms scan their entire book in plain language for growth and service opportunities. Advisors can ask which clients hold an old-employer 401(k) to consolidate, or which have a review due with more than $500,000 in held-away assets, and get matching lists in seconds. QuantumStreet AI Launches a 130/30 AI-Driven Global Equity Strategy

QuantumStreet AI, an IBM partner, launched a long-short global equity strategy built on a 130/30 framework — 130% long and 30% short gross exposure — benchmarked to the MSCI World Index. The system blends macroeconomic, fundamental, and technical signals with unstructured inputs such as news flow, earnings-call transcripts, and regulatory filings. The firm reported backtested annualized returns of 22.9% from February 2012 through November 2025, versus 11.5% for the index, and says its strategies now hold roughly $7.5 billion in client assets.

Why it matters: Backtested outperformance is not realized return, and a 130/30 AI strategy concentrates model risk in both the long and short books. Gatekeepers evaluating AI-native managers should demand live track records, scrutinize how the model behaves in regime shifts absent from the 2012–2025 sample, and confirm short-side liquidity before treating it as a core equity sleeve.
