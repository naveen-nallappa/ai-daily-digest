Databricks raises $5 billion at a $190 billion valuation

Databricks closed a $5 billion round on August 13 at a $190 billion post-money valuation, led by Coatue alongside Blackstone, MGX, T. Rowe Price and new investor Sixth Street Growth. The mark is a 42% step-up from the $134 billion round in February and the company's second raise this calendar year. Advisors with clients in evergreen or interval funds holding late-stage AI names should ask managers how these step-ups flow into NAV, since a 42% remark can move reported performance without any realization.

Anthropic in talks to acquire Decart for about $6 billion

Anthropic is negotiating to buy Israeli startup Decart for roughly $6 billion, Bloomberg reported August 13, in what would be its largest known acquisition and its fifth of 2026. Decart, founded in 2023 and backed by Nvidia, builds a GPU and inference optimization stack spanning hardware-aware model design, custom kernels and proprietary compilers, alongside the Lucy real-time video model. Why it matters: A frontier lab is paying up for inference efficiency rather than more compute, which reframes the capex debate: serving costs can fall through software as well as silicon. PMs underwriting the data-center buildout should treat inference-cost compression as a real risk to power and hardware demand curves, not just a talking point.

Zeplyn agents fill Schwab account-opening forms from advisor meeting data

Zeplyn extended its Agent Nexus framework into account opening through a new integration with Schwab Advisor Center, announced August 12. On a single request, its agents pull client data already captured across meetings, emails, CRM records and documents, populate Schwab's digital account-opening fields, and return a draft for advisor review and submission. Zeplyn says pilots cut not-in-good-order submissions by roughly 80%. Folding live Schwab holdings and transactions into its AI meeting briefs saved advisors more than 12 hours a week in pilots, per co-founder Era Jain.

Why it matters: This is an agent writing into a custodian's system of record rather than summarizing meetings afterward, and it is the second vendor in three days to plug into Schwab's onboarding APIs. Firms should update supervisory procedures now: a NIGO rate that improves because an agent filled the form still needs a documented human approval step before submission.

Playbook launches an AI orchestration platform used by firms managing $660 billion

Powder rebranded as Playbook and launched an AI orchestration platform on August 13, moving from document parsing into a layer where firms build and run agents across onboarding, ACAT reconciliation, proposal generation, estate-document review, tax analysis and compliance review. Roughly 40 firms representing about $660 billion in client assets are on the platform. Founder Kanishk Parashar, who sold Real Capital Innovation to Addepar in 2021, said document-processing accuracy rose from about 33% to 97% after deploying a self-improving agent.

Why it matters: The differentiated pitch is AI unit economics — routing each task to the cheapest model that clears a quality bar, and benchmarking return per automation. That instrumentation is what most firms lack after budgeting for AI without defining success metrics, and it is a fair thing to demand from incumbent vendors before renewing seat-based contracts.
