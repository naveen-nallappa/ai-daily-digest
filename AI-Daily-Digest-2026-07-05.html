Anthropic Ships Claude Sonnet 5 at a Steep Discount to Opus

Anthropic launched Claude Sonnet 5 on June 30, its most agentic mid-tier model, able to plan and autonomously use browsers and terminals at performance close to flagship Opus 4.8. It ships with introductory pricing of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output through August 31, then $3 and $15 — well under top-tier rates. Why it matters: Cheaper near-frontier models change the build-versus-buy math for any firm running AI agents at volume. Wealth and asset managers piping models into research, drafting, or client-servicing agents should re-price their per-query costs and revisit which workloads were previously too expensive to automate, since a lower token floor makes production-scale deployment materially more affordable.

White House Nears Voluntary AI Release Standards With Top Labs

The White House is in advanced talks with OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic on voluntary standards for releasing frontier AI models, with an announcement possible within a week per July 3 reporting. The framework builds on President Trump's June 2 executive order, which asks labs to submit their most powerful models for government testing up to 30 days before public release. The order explicitly bars any mandatory licensing, pre-clearance, or permitting regime for new models.

Why it matters: Voluntary pre-release testing still inserts a government checkpoint into model launch timelines. Firms whose advisor and PM tools depend on the newest models should treat release dates as policy-sensitive and build vendor contracts and continuity plans that tolerate 30-day review windows, rather than assuming frontier upgrades ship on the labs' engineering schedule alone.

Orion (BusinessWire)

Orion Becomes First Portfolio-Accounting Provider to Earn ISO 42001 AI Standard

Orion said on June 29 it earned ISO/IEC 42001 certification, the international standard for AI management systems, after an independent audit of the governance behind its Denali AI layer. It is among the first wealthtech firms — and the first known portfolio-accounting provider — to qualify, joining roughly 100 to 150 certified organizations globally, including Microsoft, AWS, Google Cloud, and Anthropic. Denali spans CRM, planning, portfolio management, and reporting, with outputs designed to be reviewed by professionals before client use.

Why it matters: A formal AI-governance certification gives advisors a concrete diligence checkpoint beyond vendor marketing. Firms selecting an AI-enabled platform can now ask whether a provider holds ISO 42001 and how its controls handle model outputs, data, and human review — a sharper question than "do you use AI" as regulators intensify scrutiny of AI in client-facing workflows.

AQR Says Machine Learning Now Drives a Fifth of Its Flagship Signals

AQR Capital, which manages about $187 billion after a $73 billion asset gain in 2025, has moved machine learning to the core of its process, with the technique now generating roughly a fifth of the trading signals in its flagship multi-strategy fund. Investors should press quant managers on how much of returns traces to machine-learning models, how those signals are governed and explained, and whether crowding into similar data and techniques raises correlation risk across nominally distinct funds.
