Mercer Survey: 55% of Asset Managers Now Use AI in Investment Process

Mercer's 2026 AI in Asset Management Survey, published May 26 and based on 131 manager responses gathered in February and March, found 55% of firms have AI integrated in at least one investment process, 27% are in pilot, and only 18% report no integration.
