Anthropic (research)

Claude protein designs succeed against 14 of 15 targets in wet-lab tests

Anthropic published lab-validated results on August 20 showing an autonomous Claude campaign produced 354 confirmed protein binders from 1,320 designs across 15 targets, succeeding on 14. Hit rates ran 22.6% to 26.7% in a 48-hour multi-target session and 35.1% in focused 24-hour runs, against a typical 10% to 15% for protein-design campaigns. Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience independently synthesized and tested the designs, and some binders bound several times tighter than the best published results.

Why it matters: Independent wet-lab validation moves AI drug discovery from narrative to measurable benchmark, right as Anthropic courts IPO investors with science verticals in the revenue story. PMs holding AI-drug-discovery names now have a published hit-rate baseline to test manager claims against, and pharma R&D productivity assumptions in long-duration healthcare theses deserve a refresh.
